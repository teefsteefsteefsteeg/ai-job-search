A gameplay screenshot mockup of a cozy farming + house-building PUZZLE game for PC. Isometric view of a small floating square garden plot (an 8x8 grid of soil tiles with faint grid lines) on a soft pastel background. On the grid, crops are placed as chunky TETROMINO-like shaped patches: an L-shaped patch of carrots, a T-shaped patch of sunflowers, a straight 1x4 row of corn, a square 2x2 of pumpkins, a scarecrow on one tile. Small glowing +points numbers float above patches that touch each other. Beside the garden, a half-built cozy cottage made of stacked room blocks (a kitchen block and a bedroom block already placed, a translucent ghost preview of the next room block hovering where it will snap). A tiny gardener character with a watering can stands at the edge. Minimal clean UI: a tray at the bottom showing the next 3 crop pieces, a seasonal day counter top-left, a target score top-right. No real brand names, no readable long text, all UI labels are short simple icons and numbers.

ART STYLE: crisp isometric 16-bit pixel art, chunky pixels, bright cozy palette, 1px dark outlines on objects, clean readable pixel UI.
